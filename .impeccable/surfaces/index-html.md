## Direction contract

THESIS: bmiest as an instance in WoW's Adventure Guide: the overview is the stream, each project is an encounter, its links are the loot. Refuses the link-in-bio column and the hero + project-card-grid portfolio.

OWN-WORLD: flat ink ramp, jade = live/brand/selected, gold only for earned (race leader, CE), live-red only on air. Slanted ribbons with boss-head tiles as the encounter list, slanted pills as loot rows, the slanted jade primary button, flush square broadcast bug, Outfit 800 uppercase poster caps, JetBrains Mono for compared numbers, drawn SVG marks, no shadows, no rounded cards.

STORY: the visitor sees who bmiest is and that the stream is live or when it is next; takes Watch on Twitch; browses the encounters and opens one project in a click, seeing it running with two lines and its loot.

FIRST VIEWPORT: bug across the top (LIVE · bmiest · Kelderklasse · Wo+Zo 20:00, NL|EN right). Left 400px column: BMIEST in poster caps, one-line lead, the encounter list (Overview + 4 projects). Right: the entry stage, art at full stage height (overview: Shiftheal render before the guild's current boss in the jade/void glow; projects: their real screenshot in a model-viewer frame), entry title, two lines, abilities row, loot with Watch on Twitch as the primary action, visible without scrolling. Kills ticker closes the viewport edge to edge.

FORM: Adventure Guide, position 5 of 7 on the grounded list (dealt lead), seed key b3d0ab64. Signature interaction: choosing an encounter swaps the stage, the art sliding in from the model viewer's right edge with a clip-path wipe along the family slant; the hash (#overlay) makes every entry linkable. On phones every entry stacks as its own section and the list becomes a jump strip.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
