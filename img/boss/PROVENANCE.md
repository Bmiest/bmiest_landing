# Boss cut-outs: provenance

Sourced, not generated. Copied unchanged from Race to Dutch First's `site/img/boss/` (reniersworx/racetodutchfirst), which made them from Blizzard's World of Warcraft boss renders (render CDN, 600x600 JPEG on a flat ground) with `scripts/boss-cutouts.py`: ground keyed to alpha, trimmed to the body. Each PNG carries the same text and its source URL in its `impeccable:prompt` chunk. Only the current tier (The Venomous Abyss, The Tidebound Grotto) is copied; `js/bossart.js` is trimmed to match.
